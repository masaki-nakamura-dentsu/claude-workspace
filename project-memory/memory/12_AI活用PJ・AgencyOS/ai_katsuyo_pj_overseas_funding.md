The user runs (or is closely involved in) GBC's "生成AI業務活用プロジェクト" (working folder: `Work/GBC_戦略企画部/GBCインテリジェンス／知見機能/01_AI活用/Project_AI業務活用PJ/`). One active workstream (as of 2026-07-09) is persuading overseas-subsidiary presidents to budget for expatriate employees' (駐在員) generative-AI tool usage fees.

**Why this is being pushed from Japan (GBC) but paid locally:** If Japan HQ pays AI usage costs for overseas subsidiaries directly, it risks being treated as a subsidy to the subsidiary and creates a transfer-pricing / tax problem. So the local entity must bear the cost — but many local offices cut this cost to save money, leaving expats without AI access, which hurts their efficiency and leaves them with no time for local business development. GBC's ask to local presidents: fund the AI tool fees, and in exchange GBC provides hands-on training/enablement support (workshops, recorded sessions for timezone gaps, help with underused internal AI solutions like AICO2) so the investment actually gets used.

**Supporting evidence:** A survey (生成AI活用状況アンケート, first round, n=35: 20 GBC staff + 12 overseas expats + 3 domestic secondees, fielded 2026-05-25〜06-05) shows the overseas-expat group already outperforms the Japan group on nearly every usage metric — daily use 92% vs 61%, felt time-savings (top rating) 83% vs 48%, weekly reduction of 3h+ 92% vs 48% — plus concrete free-text success stories (new account wins in India, competitive-pitch wins) tying AI use directly to new business.
