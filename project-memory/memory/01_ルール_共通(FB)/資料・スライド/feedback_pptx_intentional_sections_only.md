Before finalizing any deck, check every section-divider slide and ask: is this a section I actually intended, or did it just fall out of mechanically following a structural template? Delete any divider that isn't deliberate.

**Why**: Explicitly stated as a standing, deck-wide rule (2026-09-02, [[ikeda_coaching_profile_photo_project]]). A first draft used 3 section dividers (01 目的 — covering a single content slide, 02 現状分析, 03-04 ご提案 — a divider covering two numbered Contents items at once) that didn't cleanly map to the deck's actual structure: a one-slide-long "section" and a divider labeled with two section numbers are both signs the divider was inserted to satisfy a template pattern (e.g. [[proposal_writing_framework]]'s 1-3-1 structure) rather than reflecting real content boundaries.

**How to apply**: A section divider should only exist where there's a genuine shift in topic spanning multiple content slides. Fold single-slide "sections" (like a 目的/objective slide) into the surrounding section as an unlabeled intro slide instead of giving it its own divider. If a divider's title has to mash together multiple Contents-list item numbers (e.g. "03-04"), that's a sign the Contents list and the actual divider structure have diverged — resolve by either adding the missing divider or removing the mismatched one, so Contents entries and dividers correspond 1:1. Apply this check as a final pass on any deck, not just this one.
